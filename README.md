# AGRAMI — Live Brand Marketing Project

### Brand Strategy | Consumer Research | Content Marketing | Packaging | Influencer Strategy

**Project Type:** Live Brand Project  
**Industry:** FMCG | Food & Kitchen Essentials  
**Product Categories:** Ghee, Cooking Oils & Honey  
**Focus Areas:** Brand Positioning, Digital Marketing, Content Strategy, Packaging Concepts, Influencer Marketing and Campaign Planning

---

## 1. About the Project

AGRAMI is a live marketing project focused on developing a brand and digital marketing strategy for a food and kitchen essentials brand operating across categories such as ghee, cooking oils and honey.

The project explored how AGRAMI could communicate product quality, build consumer trust, create a recognisable digital identity and connect with audiences through relevant food content and everyday kitchen moments.

The objective was to translate market and consumer insights into a practical marketing direction covering brand positioning, visual identity, Instagram content, launch planning, influencer collaborations, public relations and paid media.

## 2. Project Objectives

- Understand the ghee, cooking oil and honey categories through industry and competitor research.
- Analyse competitor positioning, communication approaches and pricing.
- Identify potential consumer segments and develop a differentiated brand positioning.
- Build a consistent visual direction and explore packaging concepts.
- Develop an Instagram strategy with clear content pillars and communication themes.
- Plan a two-month content calendar and create sample social media content.
- Propose a launch campaign supported by nano-influencers, PR and community engagement.
- Outline a Meta Ads strategy and performance measurement framework.

## 3. Research & Market Understanding

The research phase focused on understanding the food and kitchen essentials market and identifying opportunities for AGRAMI's communication strategy.

### Key areas explored

- **Industry analysis:** Ghee, edible oils and honey categories.
- **Competitor analysis:** Brand positioning, pricing and communication strategies.
- **Consumer understanding:** Research involving 63 respondents, used to explore consumer perceptions and category-related preferences.
- **Segmentation:** Potential audiences for ghee and related kitchen essentials.
- **Brand differentiation:** Opportunities to communicate product quality, sourcing and purity in a clear, consumer-friendly way.

The research informed the proposed positioning and content direction. It should be treated as exploratory project research rather than a claim of statistically representative market findings.

## 4. Brand Positioning

### Proposed communication direction

**“Prove Purity. Not Just Promise It.”**

The positioning direction centres on building consumer confidence through informative, transparent and relatable communication rather than relying only on broad quality claims.

The proposed brand communication focuses on:

- Making food-related information easier to understand.
- Communicating sourcing and product-quality stories where verified information is available.
- Educating consumers about ghee, oils and honey.
- Connecting the brand with familiar Indian cooking habits and everyday kitchen experiences.
- Building a consistent and recognisable digital identity.

*Note: This is a proposed strategic direction developed as part of the project, not a claim that the tagline or positioning was officially adopted by the brand.*

## 6. Instagram Content Strategy

The proposed Instagram strategy was built around four content pillars designed to combine product education, trust-building and relatable food content.

### Pillar 1: From Farm to You
Stories around sourcing, ingredients and the journey of food from its origin to the consumer, using verified brand information.

**Content ideas:** Meet the Source, ingredient stories and behind-the-scenes features.

### Pillar 2: Purity You Can See
Content designed to explain product quality and help consumers understand what they should look for when evaluating food products.

**Content ideas:** Purity Explained, Label Check and product education.

### Pillar 3: Know Your Food
Simple, informative content about ghee, cooking oils and honey, helping audiences make more informed everyday food choices.

**Content ideas:** Ghee 101, Food Facts, oil education and Ask AGRAMI.

### Pillar 4: The AGRAMI Kitchen
Relatable content showing how the products fit into everyday cooking, recipes and familiar kitchen routines.

**Content ideas:** My AGRAMI Kitchen, recipe Reels, home-cooking moments and food-led storytelling.

## 8. Campaign Concept — “It Starts at the Source”

The proposed launch campaign was designed to introduce AGRAMI through stories about food, sourcing, product understanding and everyday kitchen use.

### Campaign flow

**Phase 1 — Tease**  
Introduce the brand through ingredient-led visuals and short curiosity-building content.

**Phase 2 — Reveal**  
Present the brand, its product categories and the core communication direction.

**Phase 3 — Creator Content**  
Propose collaborations with relevant nano-creators to create relatable food and kitchen content.

**Phase 4 — Build Trust**  
Share educational posts, product information and sourcing-related stories supported by verified information.

**Phase 5 — Community**  
Encourage recipes, audience questions, user-generated content and participation through the proposed #MyAgramiKitchen theme.

**Phase 6 — Sustain**  
Continue with educational content, recipes, creator-led storytelling and community engagement.

The campaign was developed as a proposed launch plan, with a two-month content calendar incorporating a launch sequence and a continuing mix of education, recipes and trust-building content.

## 9. Influencer, PR & Community Strategy

The proposed creator strategy prioritised **nano-influencers, particularly creators with approximately 1,000–10,000 followers**, whose content aligns with food, home cooking, family kitchens and lifestyle.

### Influencer collaboration ideas
- Recipe Reels featuring everyday cooking.
- Product-focused kitchen content.
- Home-cooking demonstrations.
- Food and ingredient education.
- Creator-led storytelling using a natural, relatable style.

### PR & community ideas
- Sourcing-led storytelling, where supported by verified information.
- Food and recipe content for relevant media and creator outreach.
- Community Q&As and audience participation.
- The proposed #MyAgramiKitchen initiative.
- Recipe challenges, user-generated content and potential tasting or pop-up concepts.

These activities were developed as recommendations; confirmed partnerships, PR placements and campaign results are not claimed.

## 10. Proposed Meta Ads Strategy

A proposed paid-media framework was developed to support campaign awareness, audience engagement and conversion.

### Suggested campaign structure

- **Discovery — 30%:** Introduce AGRAMI and its product categories to relevant audiences.
- **Consideration — 30%:** Use educational content, product information and engaging creatives to encourage deeper interest.
- **Conversion — 40%:** Direct interested audiences towards the relevant purchase destination, subject to the availability of a suitable sales journey and tracking setup.

**Initial validation:** The plan proposed a 14-day organic content validation period before allocating paid-media budgets.

### Suggested performance indicators

- Reach and impressions.
- Video views and engagement rate.
- Link clicks and click-through rate (CTR).
- Landing-page visits.
- Add-to-cart and purchase events, where applicable.
- Customer acquisition cost (CAC) and return on ad spend (ROAS), once reliable conversion tracking and sufficient data are available.

The percentages represent a proposed allocation framework, not actual campaign spending or performance.
