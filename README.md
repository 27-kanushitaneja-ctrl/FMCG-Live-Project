# Agrami Live Project

## About the Project

The Agrami Live Project was a practical marketing project where our team worked on understanding the brand, its products, target customers and current marketing activities. The project gave us an opportunity to work on real brand requirements and develop ideas that could be used to improve the brand's communication and visibility.

We worked on different aspects of the brand including content, packaging, social media and influencer marketing.

## Project Objectives

The main objectives of the project were:

* To understand Agrami and its target audience
* To study its existing marketing and communication
* To identify areas where the brand could improve
* To develop practical content and marketing ideas
* To explore influencer collaborations
* To work on packaging and other brand touchpoints

## What We Worked On

As a team, we worked on:

* Understanding the brand and its products
* Researching the target audience
* Looking at the brand's current social media presence
* Developing content ideas
* Creating sample content
* Working on packaging concepts
* Researching influencers for possible collaborations
* Developing marketing recommendations
* Putting together the final project presentation
